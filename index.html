Help & User Guide
👁 Viewer
  • Can view all students, staff, medical, and calendar data
  • Cannot edit, add, or delete any records
  • Cannot approve or deny access requests
  • Cannot export data or change settings
✏️ Editor
  • Can add, edit and update student records
  • Can record and edit fee payments
  • Can log communication and send WhatsApp / email chases
  • Can update staff attendance and DBS records
  • Cannot approve or deny access requests for new users
🔑 Super Admin
  • Full access to everything an Editor can do
  • Can approve or deny access requests for new staff
  • Can set user roles (Viewer / Editor / Super Admin)
  • Can edit staff records including pay rates
  • Can export all data to CSV
  • Can access Google Sheets setup and configuration
🔐 Requesting Access
  1. Go to madrasah.eeis.store and sign in with your school Google account or email
  2. If your account is not recognised, you will be shown a request form
  3. Fill in your name and select the role you need
  4. A Super Admin will be notified and will approve or deny your request
  5. Once approved, sign in again and you will have access
